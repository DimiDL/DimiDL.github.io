Chrome autofill test: one user+pass login + one password-only login

Reproduces the scenario from Firefox bug 2053227: the site has two saved credentials for one origin — a normal username+password login and a password-only login. The question: with the normal login form below, does the browser silently autofill on page load, or leave the fields blank until you pick from the dropdown?

  1. Host this over HTTPS (GitHub Pages works). Open it in Chrome with the password manager enabled.
  2. In Form A, enter username firstuser@example.com and any password, click Sign in, and accept Chrome's “Save password?” prompt. (This saves credential #1: user+pass.)
  3. In Form B (no username field), enter any password, click Submit PIN, and accept the save prompt. (This saves credential #2: empty username.)
  4. Reload the page (or open it in a fresh tab).
  5. Watch Form A without clicking anything: is the username/password pre-filled on load, or blank? Then click the username field to see the account dropdown.

Form A — normal login (username + password)
Form B — password-only (PIN), used once to save the empty-username credential
